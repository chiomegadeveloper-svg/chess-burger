TO THE TRAVELER WHO OPENS THESE PAGES

Before you draw your blade

Beyond familiar roads lie hidden doors: to Biringan in Samar, Agtha beneath Biliran, and the deep halls of Agartha.

This book remembers those who crossed them and returned changed. Before they were feared, many were guardians, parents, wanderers, or children. Grief, broken promises, and desperate bargains drew them into darkness.

Read their stories before you face them. A weakness may help you survive, but understanding what was lost may reveal something no weapon can.

Even a monster once had a story worth remembering.

An original chronicle from the world of Chess Burger RPG.
