Monsters use level-based attributes with no items. GPS victories earn hero XP; device practice progress stays on this device.

One turn. One decision.

  1. Attack with one equipped skill, harvest AP, harvest mana, or defend. The action ends your turn. A full round includes both sides’ turns.
  2. All 30 skills cost AP and mana. Hover or focus an equipped skill to see its costs, damage and cooldown. The Hero Room Skill Book lists every attack and its animation. Cooldowns skip that many of your following turns. Heroes and monsters use the same skill rules.
  3. Win a full chess game as White to gain 20 AP. A loss, draw, wrong puzzle move, or abandonment gives no resource and uses your turn. You cannot harvest both resources or attack during a harvest turn.
  4. Solve every required puzzle move without a mistake to gain 20 mana. Monsters use 600–1500 rated puzzles; bosses use 1900–2400. A solved puzzle cannot reappear until at least 1,000 other successful solves. History is saved on the server for online battles, and on this device for practice.
  5. Start with 20 AP and at least 20 mana. Level 1 starts with 20 Health, 20 AP and 20 MP. Each level adds 4 Health, 2 maximum AP and 3 maximum MP. Equipment may add attributes. Gains stop at the cap.
  6. Defend restores 8 AP and 8 mana for your next turn, up to each resource cap, and halves damage during the next opponent turn. At 0 HP, you lose. Running forfeits. AP refills for each battle. Remaining mana carries forward. Remaining health carries into the next battle, even after leveling up. Drink a health potion in Hero Room to restore 20 Health, capped at maximum. Mana potions restore 20 mana. Drag a bag potion onto your hero during your battle turn to restore 20 HP or mana and end your turn. You cannot use a potion during a harvest or the opponent’s turn. Potions also work in Hero Room outside combat. Equipment and attributes stay locked until the battle ends.
  7. Every active battle has a five minute total limit, including harvests. At the limit the battle ends without victory rewards. Chess harvest has one minute per side with premoves; a flagged clock decides the harvest. Player challenges expire in 60 seconds and player turns last at most 90 seconds. Refreshing resumes online battles.
  8. Map encounters require fresh GPS and accuracy within ±10 m, plus a measured distance of 10 m or less. GPS readings are estimates. PvP also requires the other player to accept.
  9. Monster AI targets 750–1480; boss AI targets 1480–2100. Below Stockfish’s supported Elo floor, reduced skill and extra mistakes approximate the target. These training targets are not certified ratings.
  10. Monster victories drop 1–3 items, with an independent 85% weapon/armor or 15% potion chance. Each item independently rolls 85% Common, 10% Uncommon or 5% Rare. If your stash is full, throw away unwanted items before collecting the remaining drops. Stash capacity does not grow automatically.

Sandbox battles use local hero loadouts and award no competitive or monetary rewards.
