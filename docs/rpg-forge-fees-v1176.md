# Merge and forge CBG fees — v0.1176

Valid merge/forge attempts cost 18 CBG for two ingredient units, plus 8 CBG per additional unit. Three equipment items cost 26 CBG; four cost 34 CBG. Potion recipes use the number of units actually consumed, regardless of how many stacks supply them: Cola costs 18, Coconut from four Normal costs 34, mixed Coconut costs 26, and Tuba from ten Normal costs 82. Leftover stack quantities do not increase the fee. Ordinary consolidation of matching potion stacks remains free.

The fee applies to valid equipment attempts whether they succeed or fail. Failed attempts keep all ingredients. Invalid inputs, insufficient balance, unavailable transactions and inventory-capacity failures do not charge. Server-owned quotes ignore client-supplied prices. The Merge & Forge tools show the current cost, refresh the actual wallet balance, and disable unaffordable recipes. Direct drops keep their existing compact visual feedback. Confirmed charges refresh the Chess Burger wallet as well as the RPG balance.

Paid results, fee deductions, the wallet ledger and idempotency receipt commit together through the existing server-only cb_rpg_merchant_trade RPC. A stable, namespaced UUID derived from the owner and request ID prevents duplicate debits after uncertain responses. The RPC returns the saved winning receipt during a concurrent retry, so an uncommitted reroll is never returned. Existing profile revision, hero-generation and battle checks remain enforced. No database migration is required; the wallet transaction already used by the merchant is reused.
